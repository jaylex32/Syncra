║           SPOTIFY OAUTH APP - QUICK SETUP (5 MINUTES)                  ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

WHY DO THIS?
  • Get your own rate limit quota (no sharing with other users)
  • Avoid "429 Rate Limited" errors
  • 100% FREE - No payment required

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 1: CREATE SPOTIFY DEVELOPER APP
  1. Go to: https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
  2. Click "Create app"
  3. Fill in:
     • App name: Syncra Personal
     • Description: Personal playlist sync
     • Redirect URI: http://localhost:8888/callback
  4. Click "Save"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 2: GET YOUR CREDENTIALS
  1. Click on your new app
  2. Click "Settings"
  3. Copy:
     • Client ID (visible by default)
     • Client Secret (click "View client secret")

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 3: ADD TO SYNCRA
  Open: app_config.json

  Add these lines (replace with YOUR credentials):

  {
    "sp_dc_cookie": "YOUR_SP_DC_COOKIE",
    "sp_app_client_id": "YOUR_CLIENT_ID_HERE",
    "sp_app_client_secret": "YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET_HERE"
  }

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 4: RESTART SYNCRA
  Close and reopen Syncra - Done! ✓

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VERIFICATION:
  Run: python test_spotify_auth.py

  Should see:
    ✓ [SUCCESS] Token obtained
    ✓ [SUCCESS] AUTHENTICATION WORKS!

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TROUBLESHOOTING:
  • "Invalid client" → Double-check credentials (no extra spaces)
  • "Redirect URI mismatch" → Add http://localhost:8888/callback in Settings
  • Still rate limited → Delete .spotify_oauth_cache and restart
